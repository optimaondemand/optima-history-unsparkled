Primary Source Assessment

Read the sources below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE A
Grenville L. Gage, 1st Texas Infantry, letter from camp near Richmond, Virginia, 1862 (excerpt)

I have had to lie on my face in an open field all day when it appeared to me that I would melt with heat and was afraid to move hand or foot for fear of being shot all to pieces, as there was between thirty and forty cannon firing on us all day long.

SOURCE B
List of Freedmen who have been Murdered in the state of Texas since the close of the Rebellion (1866), first entries

Luke Adams. Killed by William Richard, Bosque County, June 1865. Aged 14 years. Whipped to death.

Henry Wadling. Killed by William Richard, Bosque County, Oct. 1, 1865. An old man. Whipped to death.

(Name not given). Killed by Hanson Hendricks, Hill County, July 18, 1865. Shot through heart with revolver.

QUESTION 1

Gage describes lying in a field all day under cannon fire. Using this letter and what you know about Hood's Texas Brigade, explain what the letter helps you understand about the war that a list of battles does not.

QUESTION 2

The list records killings of freedpeople in 1865 and shows no punishment. Why would a Freedmen's Bureau office keep such a list, and what does it suggest about how well freedpeople in Texas were protected?
