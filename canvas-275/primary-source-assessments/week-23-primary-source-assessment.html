Primary Source Assessment

Read the source below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE A
Barbara Jordan, statement on the impeachment of President Nixon, House Judiciary Committee, July 25, 1974 (excerpt)

Earlier today, we heard the beginning of the Preamble to the Constitution of the United States, "We, the people." It is a very eloquent beginning. But when the document was completed on the seventeenth of September 1787 I was not included in that "We, the people." . . . My faith in the Constitution is whole, it is complete, it is total.

QUESTION 1

Jordan says she was left out of "We, the people" in 1787 yet declares her faith in the Constitution "whole, complete, total." Using what you know about the fights over the vote in Texas, explain how both statements can be true.

QUESTION 2

Jordan was elected to the Texas Senate and later Congress. Name two habits of leadership from the lesson that this statement shows, and explain how.
