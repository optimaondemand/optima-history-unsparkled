Week 27 Primary Source Assessment

Read the source below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE A
J. B. Pumphrey, "The Pumphrey Brothers' Experience on the Trail," in The Trail Drivers of Texas (excerpt)

When we reached the South Fork of the Arkansas River it was night, and about five o'clock in the morning, after waking the cook, I was on my way back to the herd when I saw our horses were being hustled, and was afraid they would stampede the herd, when just then the cook yelled "Indians," and sure enough they had rounded up our horses and were going away with them. A heavy rain was falling and the boss said, "You Texas boys follow the Indians and get those horses." The two others and myself rode one day and night, having to swim rivers and creeks with our clothing fastened on our shoulders to keep them dry, making the hardest ride of my life, but we did not overtake the Indians.

QUESTION 1

Pumphrey's crew lost its horses in the night and rode a day and night to try to get them back. Using this account and the lesson, explain why a trail drive depended so heavily on the remuda (spare horses) and on good leadership.

QUESTION 2

Pumphrey's account shows a drive passing through country with little law or government. Based on the lesson, what problems could open range and weak law cause, and what later changes tried to fix them?
