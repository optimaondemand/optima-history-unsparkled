Week 25 Primary Source Assessment

Read the source below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE B
Juan N. Cortina, proclamation to the Mexican inhabitants of Texas, September 30, 1859 (excerpt)

There is no need of fear. Ordinary people and honest citizens are inviolable to us in their persons and interests. Our object, as you have seen, has been to chastise the villainy of our enemies, which heretofore has gone unpunished. These have connived with each other, and form, so to speak, a perfidious inquisitorial lodge to persecute and rob us, without any cause, and for no other crime on our part than that of being of Mexican origin . . .

QUESTION 1

Cortina says his aim was to punish enemies who persecuted and robbed Mexicans "for no other crime ... than that of being of Mexican origin." Using the lesson, explain what had been happening to Tejano families that could lead him to say this.

QUESTION 2

Texas sent Rangers under Captain Rip Ford, working with U.S. soldiers, to drive Cortina across the river. Suppose instead Texas leaders had answered his complaints about land and unequal treatment. Would the conflict have ended sooner? Explain your reasoning.
