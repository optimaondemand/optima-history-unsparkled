Read the source below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE B
John H. Robertson, letter from Meridian, Bosque County, January 22, 1858 (excerpt)

Dear Uncle . . . I have some Frontier News the Indians are Doing mischief 30 or 40 miles West & North of me they have stolen too thousand horses from the Leon Bosque & Pecon Byou Country they made there last drive that I haveve heard of about the Light of the Last Moon they killed four White men & one Negro & shot arrows into another Negro . . . ther is a gooDeal of uneasness eaven through hear about & Horses some are going Back and some are Still going west Regardless of the Indians. There was a man By the Name of Walker taken into Custody & comited to Jail in Waco for Horse stealing & a Mexican from Leon taken up by the Rangers who I expect they will hang with out a regular trial . . . there is 125 Rangers in small Companies along on the frontier from Gaits Ville & to Steavens ville and Pecon Byou and more a expected to come on.

QUESTION 1

Robertson writes that there were 125 Rangers in small companies along the frontier. Even with the army's forts, why would settlers like Robertson have wanted Rangers on the frontier?

QUESTION 2

Robertson expects Rangers will hang a Mexican prisoner "with out a regular trial." What idea about law is at stake in that sentence, and how does it connect to the lesson's two-sided view of the Rangers?
