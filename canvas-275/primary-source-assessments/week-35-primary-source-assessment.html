Primary Source Assessment

Read the sources below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE A
North American Free Trade Agreement (1994), Article 102: Objectives (excerpt)

The objectives of this Agreement, as elaborated more specifically through its principles and rules, including national treatment, most-favored-nation treatment and transparency, are to:

(a) eliminate barriers to trade in, and facilitate the cross-border movement of, goods and services between the territories of the Parties;

(b) promote conditions of fair competition in the free trade area;

(c) increase substantially investment opportunities in the territories of the Parties;

SOURCE B
Governor George W. Bush, inaugural address, January 17, 1995 (excerpts)

My guiding principle will be: government if necessary, but not necessarily government.

Texans understand governors and government do not create prosperity. Producers, inventors, and entrepreneurs do. What government does is create an environment in which people are willing to take economic risks that create jobs.

QUESTION 1

Article 102 aims to "eliminate barriers to trade." What economic idea about trade lies behind that goal, and what worry did critics raise about it?

QUESTION 2

Bush says that "governors and government do not create prosperity." Using the lesson, explain what changes in Texas politics and thinking over the previous decades help explain why a Texas governor could say this in 1995, and why not every Texan agreed.
