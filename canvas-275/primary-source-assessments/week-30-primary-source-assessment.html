Primary Source Assessment

Read the source below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE A
Governor Ross S. Sterling, proclamation of martial law in the East Texas oil field, August 1931 (excerpt)

There exists an organized and entrenched group of crude petroleum oil and natural gas producers . . . who are in a state of insurrection against the conservation laws of the state.

QUESTION 1

The governor calls oil producers a group "in a state of insurrection against the conservation laws of the state." Using the lesson, explain why pumping too much oil was a problem serious enough to call out the Guard.

QUESTION 2

Suppose Texas had left oil production entirely to the market, with no limits at all. How might East Texas and the oil industry have turned out? Explain your reasoning.
