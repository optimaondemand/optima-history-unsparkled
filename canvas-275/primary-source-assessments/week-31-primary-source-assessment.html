Primary Source Assessment

Read the source below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE A
A Texas tenant farmer's account, recorded by Dorothea Lange, Marysville, California, 1936

1927 made seven thousand dollars in cotton. 1928 broke even. 1929 went in the hole. 1930 still deeper. 1931 lost everything. 1932 hit the road.

QUESTION 1

The farmer's six lines go from "made seven thousand dollars" to "lost everything." Which idea from the lesson explains how a family could rise and fall so fast?

QUESTION 2

Suppose a New Deal program such as the Civilian Conservation Corps or farm payments had reached this farmer in 1931. What might have changed for him, and what might not have? Explain your reasoning.
