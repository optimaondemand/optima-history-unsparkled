Primary Source Assessment

Read the sources below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE A
Navy Cross citation for Doris Miller, Cook Third Class, U.S. Navy

For distinguished devotion to duty, extraordinary courage and disregard for his own personal safety during the attack on the Fleet in Pearl Harbor, Territory of Hawaii, by Japanese forces on December 7, 1941. While at the side of his Captain on the bridge, Miller, despite enemy strafing and bombing and in the face of a serious fire, assisted in moving his Captain, who had been mortally wounded, to a place of greater safety, and later manned and operated a machine gun directed at enemy Japanese attacking aircraft until ordered to leave the bridge.

SOURCE B
Mexican Farm Labor Agreement (Bracero Program), August 4, 1942, as revised April 26, 1943 (excerpts)

Mexicans entering the United States as a result of this understanding shall not suffer discriminatory acts of any kind in accordance with the Executive Order No. 8802 issued at the White House June 25, 1941.

Transportation and living expenses from the place of origin to destination, and return, . . . shall be met by the employer.

The worker shall be paid in full the salary agreed upon, from which no deduction shall be made.

QUESTION 1

The citation praises Miller for helping his wounded captain and then firing a machine gun. Why was this especially remarkable given the Navy's rules for Black sailors, and what does it suggest about the war's effect on who could serve and be honored?

QUESTION 2

The agreement promises no discrimination, equal wages, and full pay, yet Mexico kept braceros out of Texas for several years. What does this tell you about the difference between promises on paper and conditions in practice?
