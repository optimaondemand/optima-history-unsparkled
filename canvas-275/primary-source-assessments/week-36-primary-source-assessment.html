Primary Source Assessment

Read the source below. Then answer both questions in 3 to 5 sentences each, in your own words. Use what you learned in the lessons as well as what the source says. Your teacher will score your answers with the rubric on this page.

SOURCE A
U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 Census: resident population of Texas

Texas, resident population, April 1, 2020 (2020 Census): 29,145,505.

QUESTION 1

Texas had about 14 million residents in 1980 and 29,145,505 in 2020. Using the lesson, name two reasons Texas has grown so fast, and explain how each added people.

QUESTION 2

The lesson says Texas is both integrated into the nation and distinct from it. Choose one fact from the lesson that shows integration and one that shows distinction, and explain how the Census number helps show that Texas matters to the whole country.
